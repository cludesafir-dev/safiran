Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy covers "Safiran YouTube", the internal tool used by Safiran Institute to manage the YouTube channel of Dr. Javad Foroughi.

What data the app uses

The app uses Google OAuth to access only the channel owner's own YouTube account. It reads channel and video statistics and comments, uploads and edits the channel's own videos, and posts replies to comments on the channel.

How data is stored

The access token is stored privately on the channel administrator's computer and in private, encrypted automation secrets. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions.
